# Marea Groovebox: Privacy Policy

*Last updated: 8 November 2026*

Marea Groovebox does not collect, store or share any personal data.

- **No accounts, no tracking, no analytics, no ads.** The app does not contact any server on the internet.
- **Microphone:** used only while you open the MIC channel or record a sound to chop. The audio stays on your iPad and is only saved if you record it yourself.
- **Your files:** sounds you load, songs you save and recordings you make are stored on your iPad, in the app's folder in the Files app. They never leave the device unless you share them yourself.
- **MIDI:** if you connect MIDI gear, Marea sends and receives MIDI notes and clock with that gear only.

If you have a question about privacy, open an issue at https://github.com/GLAUCOMA82/marea/issues.
