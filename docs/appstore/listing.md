## Promotional text (170 max, can change any time)

Fire the siren and watch a donkey stick out its tongue as the waveform. A playable dub and rhythm box with big buttons, made for jamming.

## Description

Marea is a hands-on groovebox for the iPad: drums, bass, a chopper, sample pads, a dub siren and a tape echo you can watch, all on big square buttons. No menus to dig through. Press play and jam.

DRUMS
• 8 synthesized drum voices on coloured 16-step lanes
• 8 patterns, chain them into songs, duplicate a bar and build on it
• RANDOM to start fresh, VARY to change a few hits each pass

BASS
• 7 voices: acid, sub, reese, FM, wobble, organ and reed
• A scale-locked grid, so every note fits, with accents, slides and long notes

CHOP AND PADS
• Load or record a sound: Marea finds the tempo and cuts it on the beat
• 24 pads with their own steps; drag sounds from your library straight onto them

DUB
• A siren with five shapes and a tape echo that shows each repeat travelling along the tape
• THROW, HOLD, CRASH and DROP for live dub moves
• A live mic channel with effects to talk over the mix

MIX AND SYNC
• Meter dials, mute and solo on every channel, and a click
• MIDI clock out to keep hardware like a Volca in time
• Plays from USB and Bluetooth MIDI keyboards

16 starting grooves sorted by tempo, from dub, trip hop and downtempo through reggaeton, dembow, UK funky and grime up to jungle and drum & bass.

The header screen plays along: a gorilla drums with the kit, and when you fire the siren a donkey sticks its tongue out in the siren's own wave shape.

Everything runs on your iPad, works offline and collects no data. All sounds are made in code or come from your own files.

## Notes for App Review

Marea is a music instrument. The microphone is only used when the user opens the MIC channel or records a sound. The app runs a small web server bound to 127.0.0.1 only, which its own built-in panel uses; it does not accept connections from other devices. No account or login is needed to review it.
